## Known issues found by the final buddy review and the smoke

The smoke confirmed the kernel surface works as documented, but the final buddy review identified gaps in the renderer→kernel state synchronization that the curl-level smoke doesn't reach:

- After a successful `POST /managed-agents/spawn`, the kernel did NOT broadcast `managed-agent.spawned` over the WS bus. (Addressed in a follow-up commit.)
- The spawn response field `hooks_status` was hardcoded to `"unknown"`. (Addressed in a follow-up commit.)
- The renderer did not insert the spawn response into local state. (Addressed in a follow-up commit.)
- Settings RuntimesPanel buttons (Save/Edit/Remove) were no-ops. (Addressed in a follow-up commit.)

After these follow-up commits the workflow is end-to-end coherent.
